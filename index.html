How to read it

On paper

xG (expected goals) rates every shot by how often a chance like it is scored. Add them up and you get the quality of chances a team created — a better guide to performance than the score alone.

On paper is our verdict: if one side created at least 0.5 xG more, they "should have won". When the result disagrees, that's luck, finishing or goalkeeping — and usually the story.

Counter-attack shots are shots from fast breaks. Passes in the opposition half is the share of Posh passes played in the other team's half — how high and direct they play.

The Savage Shift compares per-game averages under Robbie Savage with Luke Williams' whole spell. Percentages change in points (57% → 30% is −27 pts).
